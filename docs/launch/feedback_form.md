# Farmer & Agriculture Student Feedback Form Outline

**Target:** 3 to 5 farmers or agriculture students testing v1.0 on mobile  
**Time to complete:** ~3 minutes

## Questions
1. **Language & Clarity:** Which language did you use? Were any words, units, or crop names confusing or unnatural?
2. **Crop Recommendation & Weather:** Did the soil/weather inputs make sense, and were the top-3 crop cards and 7-day weather alerts easy to understand?
3. **Trust & Safety:** Did seeing official government scheme links, data sources, and the KVK / Kisan Call Centre (`1800-180-1551`) note make the advice feel trustworthy?
4. **Speed & Offline Experience:** How did the app feel on your mobile connection?
5. **Missing Features:** What one feature would help you most in the next version (e.g., leaf disease photo check, soil card upload, voice input, or mandi prices)?
