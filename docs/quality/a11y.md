## 2. Issues Found & Fixed During Audit

1. **`/recommend` (`frontend/src/features/recommend/RecommendForm.tsx`)**:
   - **Finding:** Unit labels `(kg/ha)` and range helper text (`Typical: 0 – 140 kg/ha`) used `text-[#1D2B22]/60` on `#ffffff`, yielding a contrast ratio of **4.07:1** (below WCAG AA 4.5:1 for normal text).
   - **Fix:** Updated to `text-soil/80` (contrast > 8.5:1 on white).

2. **`/weather` (`frontend/src/pages/Weather.tsx`, `ForecastStrip.tsx`, `LocationPicker.tsx`)**:
   - **Finding:** Footer attribution text used `text-gray-500` (`#6b7280`) on cream `#f5f0dc` (**4.23:1**), and the `Open-Meteo.com` link used `text-leaf` (`#3b8e4f`) on cream `#f5f0dc` (**3.55:1**).
   - **Fix:** Updated footer/secondary text to `text-soil/80` and link color to `text-paddy` (`#0f4a32`, > 9.1:1 on cream) with explicit underline.

## 3. Manual Accessibility Checklist

- **Keyboard-only navigation & focus order:** Verified `Skip to main content` link is the first focusable element; `Tab` / `Shift+Tab` moves logically through header links, language selector, form controls, and footer; `Escape` closes the mobile menu and language dropdown and restores focus to the trigger button.
- **200% zoom & responsive reflow:** Verified at `360px` mobile viewport (equivalent to >350% zoom on `1280px`) with zero horizontal overflow, single-column card stacking, and >= 48px touch targets on primary controls.
- **Semantic landmarks & screen reader ergonomics (`/recommend`, `/schemes`):**
  - Proper `<header>`, `<nav aria-label="Main Navigation">`, `<main id="main-content">`, and `<footer>` landmarks.
  - All inputs on `/recommend` have explicit `<label htmlFor="...">` associations, unit descriptions, and `aria-invalid` / `role="alert"` validation errors.
  - `/schemes` search input, filter controls, pagination, and external `.gov.in` links carry clear accessible names.
- **Reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion`):** Header shadow transition (`motion-reduce:transition-none`), mobile menu transition (`motion-reduce:transition-none`), and `KrishiLoader` respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- **Color contrast:** All primary text (`soil` `#1D2B22`, `paddy` `#0F4A32` on `cream` `#F5F0DC` / white) and inverted cards (`cream` on `paddy` / `paddy-light`) meet >= 4.5:1 (measured 7.23:1–9.15:1 on `WeatherCard`).
